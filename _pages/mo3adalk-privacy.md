# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026  
**Application:** Mo3adalk (حاسبة المعدلات)  
**Package:** `hisab.mo3adal.bac`  

This Privacy Policy provides clear and transparent information regarding **iqraa** ("we," "our," or "us") regarding user information when using our mobile application, **Mo3adalk: Moroccan GPA & Bac Simulator**, and how that information is handled.

**Mo3adalk** is provided as a free application supported by advertisements and intended for use as is.

---

## 1. Local-First Design & Complete Data Privacy

Mo3adalk is built strictly on a **100% offline-first architecture**:
* **No external data transmission:** All entered grades, coefficients, calculated GPAs, exam simulator inputs, and academic targets are processed and stored locally on your device hardware (via encrypted local Android DataStore and storage).
* **No user accounts or logins:** The application requires zero registration, zero logins, and zero user profile creations.
* **No cloud syncing of academic records:** Your academic performance and school notes remain exclusively on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or any remote database.
* **Instant local deletion:** You can permanently wipe all stored subject grades and custom calculations anytime with a single tap of the reset icon.

---

## 2. Information Collection & Device Permissions

To deliver its core utility and reporting features, Mo3adalk may request minimal device permissions. These permissions are executed strictly on-device:

* **Storage and Document Sharing Access:** Required only when you explicitly choose to generate and export an official PDF report card or share your GPA card via the Android system share sheet. The PDF is compiled entirely on-device without contacting any remote rendering engine.
* **No invasive access:** Mo3adalk does **not** request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, calendar, or device identifiers beyond standard framework diagnostics.

---

## 3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

To provide this comprehensive academic tool completely free of charge to students across Morocco, Mo3adalk integrates **Google AdMob** to serve in-app advertisements (such as banner ads, interstitial transition screens, and rewarded unlock features):

* **Google Mobile Ads SDK:** Google AdMob may automatically collect and process standard technical information, including your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID), diagnostic crash logs, and general device telemetry to optimize ad delivery, limit repetition, and prevent programmatic fraud.
* **Academic Data Safeguard:** We categorically confirm that your school grades, calculated GPAs, student names on PDF report cards, and course data **are never shared or accessible** to Google AdMob or any third-party advertising network.
* For more information on how Google processes advertising information, please review the [Google Advertising Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

As an educational application designed for primary, middle school, high school, and baccalaureate students:
* We do **not** knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information (PII) from children or minors under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions).
* All user-entered academic data remains strictly on the student's physical device.
* Advertising configurations are set to comply with digital safety guidelines suitable for general educational audiences.

---

## 5. Security

We value the trust you place in using Mo3adalk to plan your academic success. Because the application operates offline-first, your personal school records never traverse the public internet, dramatically mitigating risk of exposure. We employ standard Android security safeguards to protect local preferences.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new educational features, or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage users to check this page periodically for updates.
